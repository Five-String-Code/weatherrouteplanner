Privacy Policy - Weatherline

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Weatherline ("the app") is published on Google Play by Five String Dev, operated by Five String Code LLC, 2000 Northampton Dr, Conroe, TX 77303, United States ("we", "us"). Website: fivestringcode.com. Package name: com.fivestringcode.weatherline. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and who receives it.

Location data

The app collects your device's precise location, only after you tap "Agree" on an in-app disclosure and grant the Android location permission. It is used to:

  • Set your current location as a trip's starting point, if you choose to use it.
  • Show your live position on the map during an active trip and update arrival-time estimates and forecasts for the waypoints ahead of you.

Location is collected while the app is open and while a trip is being tracked, during which Android shows an ongoing notification. The app does not collect location when it is closed. Your location is not sent to us. We do not use it to target ads. It is sent only to the third-party services listed below, as coordinates, to plan your route and fetch forecasts.

Third-party services

To plan routes and fetch weather, the app sends coordinates or search text to these public services:

  • OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) - road routing between your origin, destination, and waypoints.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) - address search and reverse geocoding.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) - weather forecasts for each waypoint.
  • RainViewer - animated precipitation radar tiles.

Each operates independently with its own privacy practices; we do not control how they log or retain requests.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob, a Google service, may collect and use your device's advertising ID (also called the Android Advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and for analytics. We do not receive this data from AdMob for any purpose other than showing ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). For how Google handles this data, see Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Data storage and retention

Trips you plan are saved locally on your device only, so the app can restore them the next time you open it. We do not operate a server, do not run our own analytics, and do not have access to your trips, location, or search history. Uninstalling the app deletes the locally stored trips.

Your choices

You can decline the location disclosure or revoke the location permission at any time in Android settings; planning routes by typing addresses still works without it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
