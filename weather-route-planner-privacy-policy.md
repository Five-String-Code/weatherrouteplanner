# Privacy Policy — Route Weather Planner

*Last updated: August 6, 2026*

Route Weather Planner ("the app") is developed by Five String Code. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

## Location data

The app uses your device's location to:

- Set your current location as a trip's starting point, if you choose to use it.
- Track your live position during an active trip, so it can update arrival-time forecasts for the waypoints ahead of you as you drive.

Location tracking runs only while a trip is actively planned and only for as long as the app is used for that purpose. It is not collected, stored, or transmitted to us — it stays on your device and is sent only to the third-party services below as needed to plan your route and fetch forecasts.

## Third-party services

To plan routes and fetch weather, the app sends coordinates or search text (never any personal identifier) to these free, keyless public services:

- **OSRM** (router.project-osrm.org) — road routing between your origin, destination, and waypoints.
- **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap** (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) — address search and reverse geocoding (turning coordinates into place names).
- **Open-Meteo** (api.open-meteo.com) — weather forecasts for each waypoint.
- **RainViewer** — animated precipitation radar tiles.

Each of these operates independently and has its own privacy practices; we don't control how they log or retain requests. We don't send them any information beyond what's needed to answer the specific request (e.g., a pair of coordinates or a search string).

## Data storage

Trips you plan are saved locally on your device only, so the app can restore them the next time you open it. We don't operate a server, don't collect analytics, and don't have access to your trips, location, or search history.

## No accounts, no ads

The app doesn't require an account and doesn't show ads.
